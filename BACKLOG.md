# Training Tracker: features for later

Saved 26 Nov 2026. Bring these up once features 1–5 are finished (supersets for any exercises, warm-up sets, PR alerts, "to beat" targets, per-exercise rest).

| # | Feature | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Weekly sets per muscle group | Shows whether chest, back, shoulders and so on get balanced volume across the split (Liftosaur does this) |
| 7 | Calendar and consistency | Workouts per week, streaks, and a calendar view |
| 8 | Body weight log | Gives pull-ups and chin-ups real context, since bodyweight is the load; synced to a Body tab in the sheet |
| 9 | Edit past workouts in the app | Instead of editing the Google Sheet directly |
| 10 | Plateau flag | Marks an exercise with no progress in about 3 sessions |
| 11 | Plate calculator | Plates per side for plate-loaded machines |
| 12 | Workout summary card | A shareable image with time, volume and PRs |

## Decided against

- **RPE / reps-in-reserve:** every working set goes to failure.
- **Lock-screen rest timer, Apple Watch:** a web app can't do these; they need a native iPhone app.
- **AI-generated workouts, exercise videos:** not needed with your own split.
